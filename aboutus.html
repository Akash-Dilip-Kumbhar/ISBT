About ISBT Kumedi
Regional Transit Infrastructure

Developed by the Indore Development Authority (IDA), ISBT Kumedi represents the pinnacle of multimodal transit engineering and high-footfall commercial planning in Central India.

IDA CIVIC MANDATE

Transforming Regional Mobility & Urban Commerce

As Indore solidifies its benchmark status as the cleanest city of India and a surging economic epicenter, the regional transit demands of Sarwate and Navlakha have reached infrastructure capacity thresholds.

ISBT Kumedi is an engineered solution designated 16.5 acres on the MR-10 junction to handle over 1,200 buses daily with pre-planned passenger concourses, airport-standard departure overheads, and 130+ prime commercial spaces.
